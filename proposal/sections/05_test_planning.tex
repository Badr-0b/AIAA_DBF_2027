\section{Test Planning}
Tests are ordered by the risk they retire and verify the SR column (Table~\ref{tab:tests}); the Fowler test (TC-09, 15 Jan) measures trimmed $C_{L,\max}$ in prototype stalls and cycles the tracks 100 times. Flights build up from taxi and a light, trimmed first flight through stalls and flaps to payload in steps, and stop if control or CG margins, temperatures or the pack reserve are off. The prototype logs at least 20 flights before freeze, each on the checklist below; the weigh-in also checks the span, and TC-14 runs by 10 April. Instruments: thrust-stand load cell, drop-rig accelerometer, ESC telemetry over ExpressLRS (no GPS), video lap timing, scales, CG rig. Top risks (likelihood, consequence): Fowler underdelivers (M, H; TC-09, else B), dart swing (M, H; TC-01), stow failure (H, M; TC-05), fuse opening (L, H; TC-02).
