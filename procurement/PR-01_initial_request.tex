\section{How to Read This Request}
Each table covers one category. Every line gives the quantity, unit price and extended price in US dollars, plus two status words:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{BUY}: the specification is fixed; order now.
\item \textbf{HOLD}: reserve the budget now; the exact part follows the sizing gate.
\item \textbf{listing}: the vendor's listed price on 9~Oct~2026. It is a reference, not a quote.
\item \textbf{estimate}: a planning figure that has not been verified.
\end{itemize}
Prices exclude shipping, duties and VAT. Dinar figures use \prRateKWD{}~KWD per US dollar, and the one sterling listing uses \prRateGBP{}~USD per pound (market rates, 8~Oct~2026). \section{Purchasing Constraints From the Rules}
These come from the 2026--27 contest rules (September 2026 draft). A substitution that breaks one of them can fail tech inspection, so please check with the team before swapping a part on these lines.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Radio.} Use ExpressLRS 2.4\,GHz receivers with PWM outputs (team decision, 8~Oct~2026). CRSF-only receivers need a flight controller, and flight controllers are banned (§3.2.1, §4.2.1). Buy FCC versions: the radio must use a legal US frequency (§3.2.1).
\item \textbf{Batteries.} Every pack (propulsion, receiver and sensor) must be unaltered, commercial off-the-shelf, and carry the manufacturer's label stating chemistry, capacity, voltage and C-rating (§3.2.3, §3.1.1).
\item \textbf{Propulsion packs.} No pack may exceed 100\,Wh, and neither may the total on the airplane (§3.2.3.2). Keep the original packaging: tech inspection checks it (§4.2.3). Lithium packs ship as dangerous goods, so buy them locally if possible.
\item \textbf{Charging sacks.} Commercial, unaltered and manufacturer-labelled. Every battery is stored and charged in one (§3.2.3).
\item \textbf{Fuses.} Blade fuses in a separate harness. The rating may not exceed the pack's maximum continuous current or 100\,A (§3.2.3.1, §3.2.3.2).
\item \textbf{Arming plug.} Its wire must match the pack leads' gauge or be heavier (§3.2.2). The reference pack has 10\,AWG leads.
\item \textbf{ESCs.} The BEC must be disabled, and a separate receiver battery is mandatory (§3.2.3, FAQ A.3).
\item \textbf{Motors, propellers, electronics.} Use unmodified commercial motors and commercial propellers. The airplane may carry no camera, GPS, autopilot or flight controller (§3.2.1).
\item \textbf{Latches.} Positive mechanical latches only; magnets are not acceptable (§4.2.1).
\end{itemize}
