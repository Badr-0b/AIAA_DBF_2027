SCREENING (before the trade):
  D   Drum with the payload receiver riding on it (no slip ring): The rules allow a payload Rx anywhere in the airplane (Sec. 3.1.1), but the radio system needs an "externally accessible SWITCH" (Sec. 3.2.1), and tech checks that "the airplane does not respond to commands with receiver switch OFF" (Sec. 4.2.5). A self-powered Rx on a spinning drum fails both unless its power comes through a rotating joint, which brings the slip ring back.
  X1  Telescopic boom (UCF 2021): The sensor must connect "using a tow line" and deploy >= 1.5 x span (2.74 m) from the exit (Secs. 3.1.1, 3.1.2). UCF's 42.5 in (1.08 m) rods met the 2021 rule (10 x sensor length); a 2.74 m boom does not fit, and a boom is not a tow line.
  X2  Wireless link to the sensor: "The Rx for the payload signal must be in the airplane, it may not be in the sensor"; the mode signal "must come from the airplane over the tow line" (Sec. 3.1.1).
  X4  Two-line bridle (UAB 2021 proposal): The rule reads "using a tow line" (Sec. 3.1.1), singular; two lines need a Q&A ruling and two drums, for a roll-stability gain the sensor's fixed fins already give.
  F  pulley block, 2 sheave pairs: stroke 0.74 m, actuator force 165 N at n = 1 (P16 peak-power point 250 N), payout at the P16's no-load speed 153 s against a 20 s deploy window -> out
  F  pulley block, 3 sheave pairs: stroke 0.49 m, actuator force 274 N at n = 1 (P16 peak-power point 250 N), payout at the P16's no-load speed 102 s against a 20 s deploy window -> out
  F  pulley block, 4 sheave pairs: stroke 0.37 m, actuator force 404 N at n = 1 (P16 peak-power point 250 N), payout at the P16's no-load speed 77 s against a 20 s deploy window -> out
  X3 spring reel: retracting needs >= 50 N (n = 1.5) at full deployment, but weight plus drag at n = 1 gives only 34 N to pull the line out against it; stored energy >= 148 J over 2.94 m -> out
  X5 limited-turn lead: 23 drum turns at a 20 mm drum radius, against a few turns for flat-cable clock springs -> out
  X6 sail-winch servo (position mode): 5.25 turns -> drum radius 89 mm; torque 2.99 N m at n = 1 against 1.29 N m stall -> out
  C  unbraked free-spool: the sensor falls 2.84 m to 7.5 m/s, 95 J at the stop, against 18 J the line stores at break -> C is kept only with a governor (S5)
  E  capstan: UHMWPE mu 0.1-0.2; 3 wraps hold 6.6-43 x the tail tension, so a 50 N stow needs 1.2-7.6 N from a self-tailer -> kept, with S6, S7
