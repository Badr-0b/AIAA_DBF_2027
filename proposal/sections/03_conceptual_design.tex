\subsection{Scoring Model and Sensitivity Study}
\dbfnote{About 220 words across this subsection. (1) The scoring model below. (2) How the competitor maxima are estimated, and a check that the conclusions survive if they change. (3) The findings: completion is worth 4.5 of 7.5; sensor weight appears in M2, M3 and GM; GM is drop energy with height capped at 60 in; container size sets simulator size and so fuselage volume. (4) Refer to Fig.~\ref{fig:sens} and Fig.~\ref{fig:carpet}.}
The total mission score is the sum of four mission scores, each a fixed completion credit plus a term normalized by the best team in the field:\footnote{AIAA, \emph{2026--27 Design, Build, Fly Rules}, Draft, Sep.~2026, Sec.~3.3.}
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
S_\mathrm{M} &= M_1 + \Bigl[1 + \tfrac{(W/t)}{(W/t)_\mathrm{max}}\Bigr]
  + \Bigl[2 + \tfrac{N W_s}{(N W_s)_\mathrm{max}}\Bigr] \\
 &\quad + \Bigl[0.5 + \tfrac{W_s h}{(W_s h)_\mathrm{max}}\Bigr]
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
