Reading:
- Script 6 draws the container from 0.4-1.0 kg. A sensor rated 100 g needs 61 mm of
  foam and a 16 L container of 0.52-1.08 kg; rated 150 g, 40 mm, 9 L, 0.42-0.81 kg;
  rated 50 g, 123 mm, 55 L, 0.80-2.04 kg. M2 carries two units.
- So the sensor's own ruggedness (potted electronics, solid ballast, a tow fitting that does not
  rely on the body shell) is the main lever on container size, fuselage bay and payload mass.
- Dropping from less than the rule maximum shrinks the container; the GM score falls in proportion
  to the declared height (GM = 0.5 + W h / (W h)max), so this is a D5 trade.
Limits: 1978 foam data (AFPEA); minimum-g reading +/- 3 G; 60 in extrapolated; flat drops on a
rigid floor only (corner and edge drops are not part of the GM); TC-02 measures our foam and box.
