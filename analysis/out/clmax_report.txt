Notes:
- Measured values are 2-D section data from the UIUC tunnel (turbulence about 0.1 %); Cl_max is the
  highest Cl of the sweep. Williamson states lift uncertainty 1.5 % and angle 0.08 deg (thesis Sec. 3.7).
- SD7037 was tested only up to Re 3e5; its 4-5e5 values reuse the 3e5 measurement (Cl_max rises
  slowly with Re for this section: +0.02-0.06 from 1e5 to 3e5).
- No wind-tunnel data exist in these volumes for SD7034 or MH 114; their clean values are XFoil
  corrected by the measured/XFoil ratio of the tested sections (method a), a weaker basis.
- These are 2-D section values. The wing's CLmax is lower (span loading, flap span, tail trim):
  wing_clmax.py.
