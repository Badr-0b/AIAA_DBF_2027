\vspace{4pt}\noindent
Badr Obtel's team has \bMembers{} members in six sub-teams organized by function, mirroring how the aircraft is scored (Fig.~\ref{fig:org}, Table~\ref{tab:roles}). The faculty advisor signs off spending and flight safety, the team lead owns schedule, budget and rules, and the chief engineer owns the configuration and alone may change a requirement. Sub-teams meet weekly on one version-controlled repository; every decision records its evidence and is reviewed at PDR (28 Nov), CDR with the A/B gate (15 Jan) and freeze (19 Feb). The pilot is an AMA member.
