\section{Decision register}
\subsection{How to read it}
Every decision in this dossier appears once here and once as a card in the body, under the same identifier. Revision~B regrades every card that rests on the flap lift estimates from \gC\ to \gE (Section~\ref{sec:revb}). The register gives the recommendation, the strength of its evidence, what would change it and when it freezes. The shaded column is left for the team to sign; nothing here is decided until it is signed.
